## Overview

**Noggin** is an adaptive learning platform built to make education more accessible, personalised, and engaging for neurodivergent children.

Education should not expect every learner to think, process information, or approach problems in exactly the same way.

Noggin takes a different approach: instead of expecting learners to adapt to a fixed educational experience, it aims to make the educational experience adapt to them.

The platform brings together artificial intelligence, adaptive learning systems, educational research, and game-based experiences to support individual learning needs.

At its centre is **Noggimigo**, Noggin's AI-powered learning companion, designed to guide learners through concepts with personalised explanations, interactive activities, and encouraging step-by-step assistance.

Noggin is an educational ecosystem built around three groups:

- **Students:** Personalised lessons, educational games, and learning progression.
- **Parents:** Insights into their children's educational development.
- **Teachers:** Tools for classroom management and student progress monitoring.

## Our Mission

We believe every child deserves access to an educational experience that recognises their individual strengths, interests, and learning needs.

Noggin aims to:

- Make personalised education accessible regardless of financial circumstances.
- Support neurodivergent learners through adaptable educational experiences.
- Encourage curiosity, independent thinking, and problem-solving.
- Give parents and educators meaningful insights into learning progression.
- Combine educational technology with research-informed learning principles.
- Make learning engaging without compromising educational quality.

## Platform Features

### 1. Noggimigo — AI Learning Companion

Noggimigo is the AI-powered educational companion within Noggin.

Its intended capabilities include:

- Personalised explanations and educational guidance.
- Socratic and step-by-step learning assistance.
- Contextual hints and feedback.
- Adaptive difficulty and lesson pacing.
- Continuity across learning sessions.
- Support for seven educational learning profiles.

Noggimigo is designed to encourage understanding rather than simply provide answers.

### 2. Adaptive Learning Engine

Noggin aims to move beyond static lesson delivery through an adaptive learning engine that responds to learner progress.

The system is designed to consider:

- Current subject knowledge.
- Responses to educational activities.
- Areas requiring additional practice.
- Lesson difficulty and progression.
- Preferred instructional approaches.
- Previous learning experiences.

The objective is to make learning responsive to the individual rather than dependent on a fixed teaching sequence.

### 3. Learning Profiles

Noggin incorporates seven learning profiles to guide educational personalisation.

| Profile | Intended accommodations |
|---|---|
| ADHD | Manageable tasks, flexible pacing, and structured activities |
| Autism | Predictable structures and clear explanations |
| Dyslexia | Multi-sensory literacy and reading support |
| Dyscalculia | Concrete mathematical representations and visual support |
| Processing | Smaller instructional steps and additional processing time |
| Visual | Diagrams, illustrations, and visual explanations |
| Kinesthetic | Interactive and hands-on learning activities |

These profiles inform educational adaptations. They are not diagnostic categories or substitutes for professional assessment.

### 4. Game-Based Learning

Noggin incorporates game-based progression to encourage participation and make educational activities more engaging.

Planned systems include:

- Interactive educational challenges.
- Experience points (XP).
- Learning streaks.
- Gems and rewards.
- Badges and achievements.
- Progression milestones.

### 5. Progress Analytics

Noggin aims to provide useful insights into learning development through:

- Student progress dashboards.
- Subject mastery maps.
- Lesson history.
- Achievement tracking.
- Learning progression reports.
- Parent and teacher insights.

### 6. Parent Dashboard

A dedicated environment for parents to understand and support their children's educational journeys.

Planned capabilities include:

- Real-time progress tracking.
- Subject mastery insights.
- Parent-focused AI assistance.
- Achievement and reward history.
- Exportable learning reports.

### 7. Teacher Dashboard

A dedicated environment for educators and classroom management.

Planned capabilities include:

- Classroom performance overviews.
- Individual student progress.
- Reward and recognition systems.
- Parent communication tools.
- Bulk data exports.

### 8. Accessibility and Safety

Noggin is being developed around accessibility, learner privacy, and responsible educational technology.

Our design principles include:

- No advertisements.
- Privacy-conscious architecture.
- Accessible interfaces.
- Inclusive educational content.
- Distraction-conscious experiences.
- Age-appropriate interactions.
- Responsible AI integration.

## How It Works

The intended learning experience follows four stages.

**01. Learner Profiling**

Establish a learner profile using relevant educational preferences, interests, strengths, and support needs.

**02. Learning Assessment**

Use gentle, interactive activities to understand existing knowledge and identify areas where additional educational support may be useful.

**03. Adaptive Instruction**

Deliver personalised lessons, explanations, and activities that respond to learner progress.

**04. Progress and Feedback**

Track educational development, provide meaningful feedback, and celebrate individual milestones.

## Technology and Architecture

Noggin is being developed with a modular architecture intended to support independent development, testing, and integration of its core systems.

The conceptual architecture consists of:

| Component | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Learning Interface | Student-facing educational experience |
| Noggimigo | AI-powered educational assistance |
| Adaptive Learning Engine | Personalisation and learning progression |
| Assessment Engine | Knowledge assessment and feedback |
| Progress System | Analytics, achievements, and learning history |
| Parent Interface | Family-facing educational insights |
| Teacher Interface | Classroom and student management |

### Engineering Principles

Development is guided by the following principles:

- **Modularity:** Independent components with clearly defined responsibilities.
- **Maintainability:** Readable, organised, and maintainable code.
- **Reliability:** Consistent behaviour and testable functionality.
- **Scalability:** An architecture capable of supporting future platform expansion.
- **Accessibility:** Inclusive experiences as a core engineering requirement.
- **Responsible AI:** Clear boundaries between deterministic educational verification and AI-generated assistance.

Implementation details, dependencies, and architectural decisions will be documented as the codebase develops.

### Research Applications

Researchers and developers may investigate questions such as:

- How can educational systems respond to individual learning needs?
- How can adaptive instruction improve the accessibility of educational content?
- How should AI-generated explanations be evaluated for educational correctness?
- How can learning progression be represented and measured?
- How can interactive educational environments support different instructional approaches?

Noggin does not claim to diagnose, treat, or cure neurological or developmental conditions.

## Privacy and Responsible AI

Because Noggin is designed for children, privacy and responsible AI are foundational engineering considerations.

Our principles include:

- **Data minimisation:** Collect only the information necessary for platform functionality.
- **Data protection:** Protect sensitive educational information.
- **Age-appropriate AI:** Design interactions appropriate for the intended learners.
- **Non-diagnostic design:** Avoid presenting educational adaptations as medical assessments.
- **Verifiable learning:** Separate educational answer verification from AI-generated explanations where appropriate.
- **Learner safety:** Prioritise accessible and safe user experiences.
